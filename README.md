# Robot Dog — Quadruped Robot for Construction Jobsite Inspection

## Overview

The Robot Dog is a quadruped robotic system developed as part of the VIP 
Robot Project, designed to demonstrate how legged robotic platforms can be 
applied to construction jobsite inspection, monitoring, and navigation tasks. 
The robot's locomotion design is informed by the operating principles of 
industry-known quadruped robots (such as Boston Dynamics' Spot), adapted here 
for a construction education and training context.

## Purpose

Quadruped robots are increasingly used in construction for site walkthroughs, 
progress monitoring, hazard detection, and navigating uneven or unstructured 
terrain that wheeled robots struggle with. Most CEM students have no exposure 
to how these systems operate before entering the workforce. This module gives 
students a hands-on, simulated understanding of quadruped robot operation in  
real jobsite conditions.

## Technical Details

- **Engine:** Unity
- **Deployment Targets:** VR devices
- **Software/Languages:** Unity (C#)
